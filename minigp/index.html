Mario Kart Mini GP

Mario Kart Mini Grand Prix, as the name suggests, is a small mod for Mario Kart DS. This mod contains 4 new original tracks and 4 retro tracks originating from the Super Circuit. The mod also includes 3D models for the kart shells, 4 new characters, 1 character never before seen in any Mario Kart mod, and several new karts. The mod also features two new items: the Thundercloud and the P Wing.

European ROM only!
